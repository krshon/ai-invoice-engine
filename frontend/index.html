About TrustInvoice

Fighting invoice fraud
with artificial intelligence

Every year, businesses lose billions to fraudulent invoices. TrustInvoice was built to give finance teams an instant, reliable second opinion — powered by AI.

What it does

TrustInvoice analyses uploaded invoice documents using multi-layer AI inspection — checking structure, metadata, fonts, numbering patterns, and image integrity to produce a fraud risk score.

How it works

Upload a PDF, JPG, or PNG. The AI engine runs structural analysis, cross-checks metadata timestamps, verifies font consistency, and inspects visual layers — all in under two seconds.

What you get

A clear verification score from 0–100, colour-coded risk indicators, a plain-language explanation of every finding, and downloadable PDF/JSON reports for your records.
